Importance of Donation
Swipe ↔️

Agni Purana

"Just by starting the construction of a temple of Lord Krishna, Sins committed in Seven Births will be wiped out and one will deliver his forefathers who are suffering in the hellish planet"

Bhagavad Gita 9.26

"If one offers Me with love and devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit or water, I will accept it."

Srimad Bhagavatam 11.27.49

"By installing the Deity of the Lord, one becomes king of the entire earth, by building a temple for the Lord, one becomes ruler of the three worlds..."

Srila Prabhupada

"By constructing a temple for Krishna, one earns millions of times more merit than by performing sacrifices, and secures a path back to Godhead."

Skanda Purana

"Whoever donates even a single brick for the construction of Vishnu's temple is elevated to the spiritual sky and freed from all past karma."
