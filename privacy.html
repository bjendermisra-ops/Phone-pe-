PRIVACY POLICY

ISKCON BHUVAIKUNTHA
Effective Date: 30 August 2026
Last Updated: 30 August 2026

International Society for Krishna Consciousness (“ISKCON”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects the privacy of devotees, donors, visitors, and users of the ISKCON Bhuvaikuntha mobile application, website, and related digital services (collectively, the “Services”).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how we use it, when it may be shared, how it is protected and retained, and the choices available to users.

This Privacy Policy applies to the ISKCON Bhuvaikuntha application on Android and iOS, as well as associated web pages and digital services operated in connection with the application.

By using the Services, you acknowledge the privacy practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where consent or device permission is required, it will be requested separately through the appropriate application or operating-system interface.

1. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

The information we collect depends on the features you choose to use. We may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Mobile number
  • Full name / legal name
  • Email address
  • Profile photograph
  • Postal address
  • City
  • State
  • PIN/postal code
  • PAN, where provided for applicable donation/tax documentation
  • Gotra, where voluntarily provided for Sankalpa
  • Donation and Seva information
  • Donation amount
  • Donation category
  • Transaction/reference information
  • Payment status
  • Donation receipt information
  • Technical and device information
  • Notification-related information
  • Application session information
  • Information voluntarily provided when contacting us or using application features

We do not necessarily collect every category listed above from every user. Information is collected according to the features used by the user.

2. MOBILE NUMBER LOGIN

ISKCON Bhuvaikuntha currently provides account/login functionality using a mobile number. Your mobile number may be used to:

  • Identify your account
  • Sign you into the application
  • Verify your identity where required
  • Associate your devotee profile with your account
  • Associate eligible donation records with your account
  • Maintain account security
  • Recover or verify access where supported
  • Communicate important account or service information

Where mobile-number verification is required, a verification mechanism such as an OTP may be used.

We do not ask users to provide biometric information such as fingerprints or facial scans for login.

3. LOGIN SESSION AND REMEMBERED SIGN-IN

After a successful login, the application may securely maintain a login session or authentication state on your device so that you do not need to sign in every time you open the application.

This may involve the use of secure authentication tokens, session identifiers, local application storage, or other authentication mechanisms necessary to maintain the signed-in state.

These mechanisms are used for authentication and application functionality. Users may be required to sign in again if, for example:

  • They log out;
  • The authentication session expires;
  • Application data is cleared;
  • The application is reinstalled;
  • The account is changed;
  • A security-related re-authentication is required; or
  • The authentication system otherwise requires verification.

The application does not use biometric authentication as part of the current login flow.

4. DEVOTEE PROFILE INFORMATION

Users may create or maintain a devotee profile. Depending on the information provided by the user, the profile may contain:

  • Full name / legal name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Profile photograph
  • PAN
  • Full postal address
  • City
  • PIN/postal code
  • State
  • Gotra
  • Other information voluntarily provided through available profile fields

Profile information may be used to:

  • Maintain the devotee's account
  • Provide personalized application functionality
  • Facilitate donations
  • Generate applicable donation records
  • Facilitate applicable tax documentation
  • Provide requested devotional services
  • Communicate with the user
  • Provide support
  • Maintain administrative records where necessary

Users should provide accurate information where the information is required for receipts, tax documentation, donations, or other official records.

5. PROFILE PHOTOGRAPH

Users may choose to add or change a profile photograph.

The application may use an operating-system photo picker, media picker, camera, or other permitted mechanism for selecting or capturing an image.

Where device permission is required, the application will request the relevant permission.

Photo access is intended to be used only for functionality initiated by the user, such as selecting or uploading a profile photograph or other supported content.

Users may deny or revoke applicable camera/photo permissions through their device settings. If permission is denied, the related photo functionality may not be available.

6. CAMERA ACCESS

If the user chooses a feature that requires taking a photograph, the application may request camera access.

Camera access will only be used for the functionality for which access is requested.

ISKCON Bhuvaikuntha does not intentionally activate the device camera in the background for unrelated monitoring or surveillance.

Users can revoke camera permission through their Android or iOS settings.

7. GOTRA AND SANKALPA

The application may provide an optional field for users to enter their Gotra for Sankalpa or other relevant devotional purposes.

Where Gotra is optional, users may choose whether to provide it. Gotra information may be used for:

  • Sankalpa
  • Requested religious/devotional services
  • Related temple administration where reasonably necessary

Gotra information is not collected for behavioral advertising. We do not sell Gotra information to advertisers or data brokers.

8. DONATIONS AND SEVA OFFERINGS

ISKCON Bhuvaikuntha may provide facilities for devotees to make donations and participate in Seva offerings. Available offerings may include, for example:

  • Bricks Seva
  • Cement Seva
  • Temple construction Seva
  • Other temple Seva
  • Devotional campaigns
  • Preset donation amounts
  • Custom donation amounts
  • Other donation opportunities displayed within the Services

Available Seva offerings and donation amounts may change from time to time. When you initiate or complete a donation, we may process information such as:

  • Donor name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Donation amount
  • Seva/donation category
  • Date and time
  • Transaction/reference identifier
  • Order/reference number
  • Payment status
  • Receipt information
  • Donation history
  • Information required for reconciliation
  • Information required for tax or accounting purposes

Donation records may be associated with your account where necessary to provide donation history, receipts, support, tax documentation, or other donation-related functionality.

9. DONOR INFORMATION

Before completing a donation, users may be asked to confirm or provide donor information. This may include:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • PAN, where applicable
  • Address, where applicable
  • Other information necessary for receipts or applicable tax documentation

Information already available in a user's devotee profile may be used to pre-fill eligible donation fields for convenience.

Users should verify the information before completing a donation.

10. PAN INFORMATION

The application may allow users to provide a Permanent Account Number (PAN), particularly where the donor wishes to receive applicable tax-related donation documentation or benefits. PAN may be processed for purposes including:

  • Donor identification
  • Eligible donation documentation
  • Applicable 80G documentation
  • Form 10BE or other applicable tax documentation
  • Accounting
  • Audit
  • Statutory record keeping
  • Compliance with applicable Indian tax or regulatory requirements

PAN is not collected for behavioral advertising. We do not sell PAN information. Where PAN is not required for a particular transaction or legal purpose, users should not be required to provide it unless otherwise necessary under applicable requirements.

11. 80G AND FORM 10BE

Where eligible and applicable, users may request tax-benefit documentation in connection with donations. The application may provide an option relating to Income Tax benefits, 80G receipts, Form 10BE, or other applicable donation documentation. For this purpose, information such as the following may be processed:

  • Legal name
  • PAN
  • Address
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Donation amount
  • Donation date
  • Transaction details
  • Other information required under applicable law

Tax benefits are subject to applicable law, eligibility, and the requirements applicable to the relevant donation and organization.

12. PAYMENT PROCESSING

Payments made through ISKCON Bhuvaikuntha are processed through authorized third-party payment service providers/payment gateways.

The payment interface currently made available through the application may include PhonePe or another authorized payment processor selected by ISKCON.

Depending on availability and the applicable payment provider, payment options may include:

  • UPI
  • Debit card
  • Credit card
  • Net banking
  • Other supported payment methods

When you proceed to payment, you may be redirected to or shown an interface operated or facilitated by a third-party payment processor. Payment authorization is performed through the applicable payment provider, bank, card network, UPI service, or other financial service involved in the transaction.

13. PAYMENT CREDENTIALS

ISKCON Bhuvaikuntha is not intended to directly collect or store sensitive payment authentication credentials such as:

  • UPI PIN
  • Complete debit/credit card number
  • CVV
  • Internet banking password
  • Banking PIN
  • Payment OTP used to authorize a transaction

Such information is entered into or processed through the applicable payment provider, bank, card network, or financial institution. Users should never disclose their UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV, banking password, or payment OTP to anyone claiming to represent ISKCON.

14. PAYMENT AND TRANSACTION INFORMATION WE MAY RECEIVE

Although sensitive payment credentials are processed by the applicable payment provider, ISKCON may receive or retain transaction-related information necessary to manage donations. This may include:

  • Transaction ID
  • Order ID
  • Payment reference number
  • Donation amount
  • Payment status
  • Date and time
  • Payment method/category, where provided
  • Successful/failed/pending status
  • Refund information, where applicable
  • Reconciliation information

This information may be used for confirming donations, issuing receipts, maintaining donation history, resolving payment issues, accounting, reconciliation, audit, fraud prevention, and tax/regulatory compliance.

15. THIRD-PARTY PAYMENT SERVICES

Payment providers operate under their own terms, security practices, and privacy policies. When users interact with a payment provider, information processed directly by that provider may be governed by the provider's privacy policy and terms.

ISKCON is not responsible for independent processing performed by third-party payment providers outside ISKCON's control. Users are encouraged to review the relevant payment provider's terms and privacy information.

Users may deny or revoke location permission through their device settings. The application does not use an IP address as a substitute for undisclosed continuous precise-location tracking.
